# Bilingual portfolio content

- Keep all visitor-facing French and English content in `src/main.tsx`'s `copy.fr` and `copy.en` entries, and keep their keys in sync.
- Whenever copy or a content-driven UI label changes in one language, translate and adapt the corresponding entry in the other language in the same change.
- When adjusting shared layout or styles to fit localized content, check both language versions and mobile layouts.
- In French copy, retain the English role titles “UX designer” and “researcher”; do not translate them as “designer UX” or “chercheuse”.
